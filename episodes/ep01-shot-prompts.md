# 第 1 集《夸夸贴纸》— 逐镜头画面提示词

用法：
1. 先上传本镜头出场角色的参考图（小米：`characters/xiaomi-kid-reference.png`；表哥：`characters/biaoge-reference.png`；奶奶：`characters/nainai-reference.png`）。
2. 粘贴 **通用前缀 + 本镜头提示词**。
3. 有需要时加上 **通用反向提示词**。
4. 图生视频时，把“动作”一栏填进视频模型的运动描述。画幅统一 16:9。

**通用前缀：**

> 2D family sitcom cartoon, thick uniform dark purple-black outlines with a slightly wobbly hand-drawn line, flat colors with hard-edged cel shadows, no gradients, no texture, 16:9 frame.

**角色简写**（替换进下面的提示词）：

- 【小米】= a 10-year-old Chinese girl with short black chin-length hair and one springy tuft on top, bright red hoodie, navy track pants, white sneakers
- 【表哥】= a round chubby young Chinese man, bald with a single curly hair on top, huge round thick black glasses, half-closed deadpan eyes, light grey-blue pajama top under a black quilted puffer vest
- 【奶奶】= a tiny Chinese grandma around 80, white hair in a round top bun, thin gold-brown round reading glasses, maroon Chinese padded jacket with gold frog buttons
- 【贴纸】= a round glossy golden smiley-face sticker with a tiny sparkle

**通用反向提示词：**

> remote control, 3D render, gradients, realistic, anime sparkly eyes, extra fingers, text, watermark
